🎯 Recommended for College Accounts (@vishnu.edu.in)
College domains often block third-party OAuth apps from sending emails. A 16-character Google App Password connects directly to Gmail SMTP with 100% reliability and zero scope restrictions.
How to get your 16-character App Password (in 1 minute):
1. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
2. Enter App name: ECell Agent and click Create.
3. Google will show a 16-letter password (e.g. abcd efgh ijkl mnop). Copy and paste it below.

Quick Google Cloud Setup:
  1. Go to Google Cloud Console.
  2. Enable Gmail API.
  3. Create OAuth 2.0 Web Client with redirect URI: http://localhost:3000/api/auth/google/callback.
  4. Paste your Client ID & Secret above and click "Save & Sign in with Google".
